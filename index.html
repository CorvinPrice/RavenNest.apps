What it does

RavenShare creates a local Wi‑Fi Direct connection and routes the connected Android device through a private tunnel to the sharing phone.

Free and Pro

  • Free: ad-supported, with daily full-speed sharing time and receiver sessions.
  • Pro: no ads and no free-version limits.
