RavenShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

RavenShare and RavenShare Free are Android apps by RavenNest. They help one Android device share its internet connection with another Android device over a local Wi‑Fi Direct connection and a private tunnel.

Data we collect

RavenNest does not operate a server that receives your browsing traffic. The tunnel runs between your devices and exits through the sharing phone's own internet connection.

The apps may store local settings on your device, such as the network name, connection state, usage counters for the free version, and cryptographic keys needed to establish the tunnel. This information stays on the device unless you choose to share it with support.

Advertising in RavenShare Free

RavenShare Free includes advertising provided by Unity Ads. Unity Ads may collect device and advertising identifiers and other information needed to serve and measure ads, according to Unity's own privacy policy and terms.

Permissions

The apps request permissions needed to create or join the local Wi‑Fi Direct connection, run the VPN tunnel, keep the connection alive in the background, and check for updates. Android may require location permission for Wi‑Fi Direct discovery; RavenShare does not use location to track where you are.

Children

RavenShare is a network utility and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
